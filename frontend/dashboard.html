About Harvestment

An Intelligent Agricultural Forecasting System for Gujarat

Harvestment combines RandomForest + GradientBoosting ensemble models to deliver yield predictions, ROI analysis, and scenario planning across 29 crops and 32 districts of Gujarat — with live weather integration powered by Open-Meteo.
